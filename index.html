About Me

I’m a Business Intelligence & Process Improvement Analyst specializing in data analytics, Power BI, Excel, SQL, KPI development, and process improvement.

I turn business data into clear insights that help organizations understand performance, identify opportunities, improve processes, and make better decisions. My experience includes developing dashboards and reports, analyzing operational and financial data, designing performance metrics, and delivering data-driven solutions for business needs.

I take an end-to-end approach to analytics—from understanding the business problem and preparing the data to analysis, visualization, insights, and recommendations.

I’m open to part-time, project-based, freelance, consulting, and full-time opportunities in Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, Reporting, and Process Improvement.

Every project in this portfolio follows a structured analytics workflow—from understanding the business problem to delivering actionable recommendations.
