Executive Summary

This project analyzes food and beverage sales data to uncover revenue trends, product performance, profitability, and customer purchasing behavior. Through an interactive Power BI dashboard, the project transforms raw sales data into actionable insights that support performance monitoring, product evaluation, and data-driven business decision-making.

Dashboard Preview

Business Context

Food and beverage businesses operate in highly competitive environments where revenue, product performance, profitability, and customer purchasing behavior directly influence business performance. Centralized sales reporting enables decision-makers to monitor performance, identify opportunities, and support more informed commercial strategies.

Business Challenge

  • Which products generate the highest sales?
  • Which products contribute most to overall business performance?
  • How is revenue distributed across products and categories?
  • Which products demonstrate stronger profitability?
  • What customer purchasing patterns can be identified?

Project Objectives

  • Analyze overall food and beverage sales performance.
  • Evaluate revenue and product performance.
  • Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
  • Analyze profitability across the product portfolio.
  • Understand customer purchasing behavior.

Executive Findings

Product performance varies across the portfolio.

Analyzing sales performance highlights differences between products and helps identify products that contribute more strongly to overall business performance.

Revenue performance provides visibility into commercial opportunities.

Monitoring revenue across products and sales dimensions enables decision-makers to identify stronger-performing areas and evaluate opportunities for improving overall sales performance.

Profitability provides an additional perspective beyond sales volume.

Evaluating profitability alongside sales performance helps distinguish products that generate strong commercial value from products that may require further review.

Business Recommendations

  • Prioritize high-performing products in sales and promotional strategies.
  • Monitor product profitability alongside revenue performance.
  • Review underperforming products to identify improvement opportunities.
  • Use customer purchasing insights to support product and sales planning.
