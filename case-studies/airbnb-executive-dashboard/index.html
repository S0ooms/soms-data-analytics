Hospitality Analytics

🏠 Airbnb Executive Dashboard

Executive Business Intelligence Case Study

Executive Summary

This project analyzes Airbnb listings across global cities to uncover pricing trends, neighborhood performance, customer satisfaction, and market opportunities. Through interactive Power BI dashboards, the project transforms raw marketplace data into actionable insights that support strategic pricing, investment planning, and executive decision-making.

Dashboard Preview

Business Context

Airbnb hosts and property managers operate within highly competitive markets where pricing, occupancy, guest satisfaction, and location all influence business performance. Decision-makers require centralized reporting to identify opportunities and optimize marketplace strategies.

Business Challenge

  • Which cities command the highest listing prices?
  • Which neighborhoods generate the greatest activity?
  • How competitive is each market?
  • How does customer satisfaction compare across cities?
  • Where should investment be prioritized?

Project Objectives

  • Analyze global Airbnb marketplace performance.
  • Compare pricing across cities.
  • Evaluate guest satisfaction.
  • Identify high-performing neighborhoods.
  • Support executive decision-making.

Executive Findings

Pricing differs significantly across cities.

Cape Town and Bangkok command substantially higher listing prices than most other cities.

High-density neighborhoods dominate listing activity.

Centro Storico contains the highest concentration of listings within the marketplace.

Customer satisfaction remains consistently high.

Cities maintain review scores above 9.0 across multiple service dimensions.

Business Recommendations

  • Implement localized pricing strategies.
  • Prioritize investment in high-demand neighborhoods.
  • Continue monitoring customer satisfaction KPIs.
  • Analyze premium-priced cities for best practices.
