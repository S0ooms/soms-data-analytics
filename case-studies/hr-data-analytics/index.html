Executive Summary

This project analyzes workforce data to provide insights into employee demographics, attrition, diversity, and key human resources performance indicators. Through an interactive Power BI dashboard, the project transforms HR data into actionable insights that support workforce planning, employee retention, and data-driven HR decision-making.

Dashboard Preview

Business Context

Organizations need clear visibility into their workforce to understand employee composition, monitor attrition, and identify areas that may require attention. Centralized HR reporting enables decision-makers to evaluate workforce trends and support more informed people-management strategies.

Business Challenge

  • What does the organization's workforce profile look like?
  • Which employee groups experience higher attrition?
  • How is employee attrition distributed across the workforce?
  • What diversity patterns can be identified?
  • Where should HR focus retention and workforce planning efforts?

Project Objectives

  • Analyze employee demographics and workforce composition.
  • Evaluate employee attrition patterns.
  • Analyze workforce diversity.
  • Identify key HR performance indicators.
  • Support workforce planning and retention decisions.

Executive Findings

Employee attrition provides an important view of workforce stability.

Analyzing attrition patterns enables HR decision-makers to identify employee groups and workforce areas that may require additional retention attention.

Workforce demographics provide visibility into employee composition.

Understanding employee demographics helps organizations evaluate workforce structure and identify patterns across different employee groups.

Diversity analysis supports a broader understanding of workforce composition.

Monitoring diversity indicators provides HR teams with additional visibility into workforce representation and supports more informed workforce planning.

Business Recommendations

  • Monitor attrition KPIs regularly to identify emerging retention risks.
  • Analyze attrition patterns across employee groups to support targeted retention initiatives.
  • Use workforce demographic insights to strengthen workforce planning.
  • Continue monitoring diversity indicators as part of HR performance reporting.
