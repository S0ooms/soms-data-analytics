Executive Summary

This project analyzes Emergency Room operations to uncover patient flow patterns, waiting times, referral trends, and operational performance. Through an interactive Power BI dashboard, the project transforms healthcare data into actionable insights that support operational planning, resource management, and data-driven decision-making.

Dashboard Preview

Business Context

Emergency Room operations require efficient patient flow, timely service delivery, and effective resource allocation. Monitoring patient waiting times, referrals, and emergency department activity enables healthcare decision-makers to identify operational bottlenecks and improve overall service efficiency.

Business Challenge

  • How efficiently are patients being processed through the Emergency Room?
  • Which factors contribute to longer patient waiting times?
  • How do patient referrals vary across the Emergency Room?
  • What patterns can be identified in ER utilization?
  • Where are opportunities to improve operational efficiency?

Project Objectives

  • Analyze Emergency Room operational performance.
  • Evaluate patient waiting times.
  • Analyze referral patterns.
  • Identify Emergency Room utilization trends.
  • Support operational decision-making.

Executive Findings

Patient waiting time is a key indicator of Emergency Room performance.

Monitoring patient waiting times provides visibility into service efficiency and helps identify areas where patient flow may be improved.

Referral patterns provide visibility into patient movement and operational demand.

Analyzing referrals helps identify how patients move through the Emergency Room and where additional operational attention may be required.

Emergency Room utilization highlights opportunities for operational improvement.

Understanding patterns in ER activity enables decision-makers to better evaluate workload, resource requirements, and overall operational efficiency.

Business Recommendations

  • Monitor patient waiting time KPIs regularly to identify operational bottlenecks.
  • Use referral trends to improve patient flow and resource planning.
  • Monitor Emergency Room utilization to identify periods of higher operational demand.
  • Use centralized dashboard reporting to support continuous operational improvement.
